# ResumeSpec Vision

> One professional identity.
> Unlimited representations.

## Introduction

The way professionals present themselves has barely changed in decades.

Today, the same information is copied and reformatted repeatedly across resumes, LinkedIn profiles, job portals, company directories, portfolios, certifications, HR systems, recruiting platforms, and AI assistants.

Every platform asks for the same information in a different format.

The result is fragmentation.

People do not have multiple professional identities.

They have one professional identity expressed through many different representations.

ResumeSpec exists to change that.

---

# Our Vision

We envision a future where every professional maintains a single structured profile that can be transformed into any representation without rewriting or duplicating information.

Instead of creating resumes, users maintain their professional identity.

Applications generate the appropriate representation whenever it is needed.

The profile becomes the source of truth.

Everything else becomes an output.

---

# A Universal Professional Profile

ResumeSpec aims to become an open standard capable of representing professional identity independently of any specific platform, company, or software.

Just as:

- HTML became the language of documents on the Web,
- Markdown became a common writing format,
- OpenAPI standardized API descriptions,

ResumeSpec aims to standardize professional profiles.

The goal is not to replace resumes.

The goal is to replace duplicated data.

---

# Beyond the Resume

A resume is only one possible representation.

The same ResumeSpec profile could generate:

- traditional resumes
- ATS-friendly resumes
- executive resumes
- one-page resumes
- multi-page CVs
- LinkedIn profiles
- personal websites
- professional portfolios
- internal employee directories
- freelancer profiles
- consulting profiles
- speaker biographies
- conference submissions
- recruitment system imports
- AI-ready structured profiles
- future formats that do not yet exist

The profile remains the same.

Only the representation changes.

---

# AI-Native by Design

Artificial Intelligence is changing how people search for jobs, recruit talent, and discover expertise.

Large Language Models perform better when information is structured, explicit, and machine-readable.

ResumeSpec is designed for both humans and machines.

Rather than forcing AI to infer information from unstructured documents, ResumeSpec provides structured data from the beginning.

AI should consume the profile.

Humans should consume its representations.

---

# Vendor Neutral

ResumeSpec is not owned by a recruiting company.

It is not tied to a specific HR platform.

It is not dependent on any résumé builder.

It is intended to remain open, portable, and interoperable.

Professional identity should belong to professionals.

Not to platforms.

---

# Community Driven

ResumeSpec is intended to evolve through public discussion and open governance.

Anyone should be able to:

- propose improvements
- discuss changes
- implement compatible tools
- build generators
- create validators
- develop editors
- extend the ecosystem

The specification should evolve through consensus rather than central control.

---

The value of the standard grows with every implementation.

---

# Long-Term Goals

Over the next decade, ResumeSpec aims to become:

- an open standard for professional identity
- a common interchange format between HR platforms
- a reliable source for AI systems
- a foundation for developer tooling
- a community-driven specification
- a language understood by both humans and machines

Success will not be measured by the number of resumes generated.

Success will be measured by the number of systems capable of understanding the same professional profile.

---

- Open

- Extensible
- Versioned
- Portable

# Looking Forward

One profile.
