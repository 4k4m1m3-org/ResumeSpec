# ResumeSpec v1.0.0 Sections

Sections are named properties inside the required `sections` object.

All sections are optional. Unknown section names are invalid unless they use the `x-` extension prefix.

## v1 Sections

- `identity`: person, contact, and location information.
- `summary`: concise professional overview.
- `experience`: professional work history or comparable professional activity.
- `education`: formal academic background using `institution`, `degree`, and `fieldOfStudy`.
- `certifications`: professional certifications and issuing organizations.
- `courses`: completed learning activities.
- `skills`: professional capabilities.
- `technologies`: tools, platforms, languages, and technical systems.
- `projects`: professional, academic, or personal projects using `links` for project URLs.
- `publications`: published professional or academic material.
- `awards`: professional or academic recognition.
- `volunteer`: volunteer activity and community contribution.
- `languages`: human languages and proficiency.
- `references`: professional references.
- `social`: professional social profiles.
- `links`: general external links.
- `achievements`: measurable professional accomplishments.
- `interests`: professional areas of interest.
- `attachments`: files associated with the profile.

## Section Containers

Section Containers are not part of the stable v1 contract. They remain an RFC concept for future consideration.
